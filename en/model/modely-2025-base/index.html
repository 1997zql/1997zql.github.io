Install in three steps

1

Download the PNG

512–1024 px, under 1 MB, already compressed for you

2

Copy to a USB drive

Create a folder named Wraps at the drive root, formatted as exFAT or FAT32

3

Apply in the car

Toybox → Paint Shop → Wraps, or upload straight from Tesla app 4.59+
